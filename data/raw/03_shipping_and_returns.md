# NovaCart Shipping and Returns Policy

**Document ID:** KB-03 | **Version:** 3.2 | **Effective:** July 1, 2026 | **Owner:** Fulfillment and Customer Experience

Membership tiers and prices are defined in KB-01. Product SKUs and warranties are in KB-05.

## 1. Shipping Zones

| Zone | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Zone A | Major metro areas in the continental US, including the 8 Same-Day metros |
| Zone B | Regional and non-metro areas in the continental US |
| Zone C | Remote areas: Alaska, Hawaii, US territories, and designated remote ZIP codes |

**Fulfillment centers:** Columbus, OH (East), Dallas, TX (Central), and Reno, NV (West). Zone C orders ship from Reno.

## 2. Shipping Tiers and Timelines

### 2.1 Delivery Timelines

| Method | Zone A | Zone B | Zone C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–5 business days | 3–5 business days | 6–8 business days |
| Express | 1–2 business days | 1–2 business days | 3–4 business days |
| Same-Day | Same day by 9:00 PM | Not available | Not available |

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US federal holidays. They are counted from the dispatch date. The day after dispatch is business day 1.

### 2.2 Same-Day Delivery

- **Metros:** New York City, Los Angeles, Chicago, Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston, Atlanta, Miami, and Seattle.
- **Cutoff:** Order before **1:00 PM local time**. Delivery is by 9:00 PM the same day.
- **Days:** Monday to Saturday.
- **Items:** Only products marked "Same-Day eligible" and in stock at the local hub.

### 2.3 Dispatch Cutoffs for Standard and Express

| Fulfillment center | Cutoff for same-business-day dispatch |
|---|---|
| Columbus, OH | 3:00 PM ET |
| Dallas, TX | 3:00 PM CT |
| Reno, NV | 3:00 PM PT |
| Zone C orders (from Reno) | 12:00 PM PT |

Orders placed after the cutoff dispatch on the next business day. An order placed Friday at 4:00 PM ET from Columbus dispatches Monday.

### 2.4 Express Guarantee and Late Deliveries

An Express order is **late** if it is delivered more than 2 business days after the end of its promised Express window.

| Tier | Remedy for each late Express order |
|---|---|
| Free or Basic | The Express shipping fee is refunded |
| Pro or Enterprise | $10.00 NovaCash credit |

If a Pro member has 3 or more late Express orders in a rolling 60-day period, they may claim a prorated refund (see KB-02 section 4).

## 3. Shipping Fees and Free Shipping Rules

| Method | Free | Basic | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $5.99, or free if subtotal is over $75.00 | $5.99, or free if subtotal is over $75.00 | Free, no minimum | Free, no minimum |
| Express | $14.99 | $9.99 | Free, no minimum | Free, no minimum |
| Same-Day | $19.99 | $14.99 | $9.99 | $9.99 |

**Rules:**
- **Free Standard for Free and Basic:** the qualifying subtotal must be **over** $75.00. An order of exactly $75.00 does not qualify.
- **Qualifying subtotal:** merchandise after promotional discounts, before tax and shipping. NovaCash redemption and gift cards do not reduce it.
- **Pro and Enterprise Express:** free on all orders with no minimum, in all zones including Zone C. Same-Day is not free.
- **PO boxes and APO addresses:** Standard only.

## 4. Returns and Exchanges

### 4.1 Return Window

- The window is **14 days from the delivery date** for all tiers.
- The return must be **initiated** (RMA requested) by 11:59 PM local time on day 14. Example: delivered Nov 2, last day is Nov 16.
- RMAs are approved within 1 business day. The item must ship back within 7 days of approval.

### 4.2 Condition Requirements

- Original packaging, all accessories, and manuals included.
- Serial number must match the order.
- Missing accessories reduce the refund by the accessory's retail price.

### 4.3 Restocking Fees

| Situation | Restocking fee |
|---|---|
| Opened electronics, not defective | **15%** of the item price paid (after discounts, before tax) |
| Sealed, unopened electronics | 0% |
| Defective or dead-on-arrival item | 0% |
| NovaCart error (wrong item, wrong color) | 0% |
| Non-electronic lifestyle items, unused and resellable | 0% |

Restocking fees apply on every tier, including Pro and Enterprise. Original shipping charges are not refunded on change-of-mind returns. They are refunded in full for defective items or NovaCart errors. NovaCash earned on a returned item is reversed.

### 4.4 Non-Returnable Items

- **Hygiene items** once unsealed. The NovaSmile Sonic Toothbrush (NC-110) and replacement brush heads are hygiene items. If opened, only defective units are eligible, through warranty or the defective path.
- **Consumables** (such as coffee pods) are non-returnable once more than 20% has been used. Sealed packs are returnable.
- Gift cards, digital licenses, custom-engraved items, and items marked Final Sale.

### 4.5 Return Shipping Fees

| Situation | Return shipping |
|---|---|
| Pro or Enterprise, any reason | Free |
| Defective item or NovaCart error, any tier | Free |
| Free or Basic, change of mind | **$6.99**, deducted from the refund |

### 4.6 Defective Items

- Report within 14 days of delivery with photos or video and basic diagnostics.
- If inspection finds no defect, the return is treated as change of mind, and restocking and return shipping fees apply.
- After day 14, a defective item is handled as a warranty claim (see KB-05).

### 4.7 Exchanges

- Same product in a different color or size: free exchange if in stock.
- A different product: treated as a return plus a new order.
- **Advance replacement (Pro and Enterprise, defective items):** the replacement ships within 1 business day of RMA approval. A hold equal to the item price is placed on the card and released when the defective unit is scanned. If it is not scanned within 10 days, the hold is captured.

### 4.8 Refund Timing

NovaCart issues the refund within 3 business days after the warehouse receives and inspects the item. The bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it. Refunds go to the original payment method.
