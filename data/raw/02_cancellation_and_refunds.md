# NovaCart Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Document ID:** KB-02 | **Version:** 3.2 | **Effective:** July 1, 2026 | **Owner:** Billing and Member Support

Plan prices referenced here are defined in KB-01. Shipping and Express definitions are in KB-03.

## 1. Cancellation Policy

### 1.1 Cutoff Window

A subscription must be canceled at least 24 hours before its renewal timestamp.

- The renewal timestamp is shown under Account → Subscription → Billing.
- Basic renews on the same day and time each month as the original charge.
- Pro renews on the same date and time each year.

### 1.2 What Happens to Remaining Days

**Canceled at least 24 hours before renewal:**
- No further charges are made.
- Paid perks continue until the end of the paid period (end of the billing month for Basic, end of the annual term for Pro).
- The account then moves to Free.
- There is no refund for unused days.

**Canceled less than 24 hours before renewal:**
- The renewal charge still processes.
- The cancellation takes effect at the end of the newly paid period.
- The 7-day refund rule in section 3 may apply to that renewal charge.

Orders already placed keep the shipping and perk pricing they were placed with.

### 1.3 How to Cancel

- In the app or web: Account → Subscription → Cancel.
- By chat or phone: Basic and Pro members can ask support to cancel.
- Canceling starts the save flow in section 6. The member can decline the offer and still cancel.

### 1.4 Canceling During a Free Trial

- Cancel at least 24 hours before day 14 and there is no charge.
- Trial perks continue until the original trial end time.
- NovaCash earned during the trial is voided.

### 1.5 Enterprise Cancellation

- Written notice is required at least 30 days before the end of the 12-month term. Otherwise the contract renews for another 12 months.
- The 24-hour rule and the 7-day refund rule do not apply to Enterprise.

## 2. Refund Matrix

| Situation | Refund | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Initial Basic or Pro charge, requested within 7 days | 100% of the subscription fee | Fewer than 2 qualifying perk orders (section 3) |
| Same, but 2 or more qualifying perk orders | $0 | Denied |
| Trial conversion charge | Treated as the initial charge | Same 7-day window and 2-order test. Only orders placed after conversion count |
| Renewal charge, requested within 7 days of renewal | 100% | Fewer than 2 qualifying perk orders since the renewal charge |
| Renewal charge, after 7 days | $0 | Non-refundable |
| Basic monthly charge, after 7 days | $0 | No partial-month refunds |
| Pro annual, canceled mid-term (no exception applies) | $0 | Perks continue to term end |
| Express Guarantee breach (Pro) | Prorated daily | See section 4.1 |
| NovaCart ends or discontinues the plan | Prorated daily | See section 4.1 |
| Duplicate or incorrect charge (billing error) | 100% of the erroneous amount | Must be reported within 90 days |
| Enterprise setup fee ($1,500.00) | $0 | Non-refundable |
| Account terminated for fraud or policy violation | $0 | Non-refundable |

Approved refunds go to the original payment method and typically post in 5 to 10 business days.

## 3. The 7-Day Full Refund Rule

A full refund is available for an initial Basic or Pro charge if both conditions are met:

1. The request is made within 7 days (168 hours) of the charge.
2. Fewer than 2 qualifying perk orders were placed since the charge.

### 3.1 What Counts as a Qualifying Perk Order

**Pro qualifying perk order:** an order that used any of these:
- Free Express shipping.
- Pro bundle pricing (15%).
- An early-access drop purchase.

**Basic qualifying perk order:** an order that used any of these:
- The discounted Express rate ($9.99).
- The discounted Same-Day rate ($14.99).
- The Basic early-access window.

NovaCash cashback alone does not make an order qualifying. Orders canceled before dispatch do not count. Returned orders still count.

### 3.2 Examples

**Pro charged Nov 3 at 10:00 AM.** The refund request deadline is Nov 10 at 10:00 AM.
- 0 or 1 qualifying perk orders: full **$180.00** refund. Perk savings already received are not clawed back. NovaCash earned in that period is removed.
- 2 or more qualifying perk orders: refund **denied**.

**Pro member at day 10 with 3 free Express orders:** denied, because the 7-day window has closed and there are 2 or more qualifying orders.

On a full refund the membership ends immediately.

## 4. Partial and Prorated Refunds

### 4.1 When Prorated Refunds Are Allowed

Only in these cases:

1. **Express Guarantee breach (Pro only).** Three or more Pro free-Express orders in any rolling 60-day period were each delivered more than 2 business days after the promised Express window (defined in KB-03). The claim must be filed within 30 days of the third late delivery.
2. **NovaCart ends or discontinues the plan,** or terminates the account without cause.

### 4.2 Formulas

- **Pro refund** = $180.00 × (unused days ÷ 365)
- **Basic refund** = $19.00 × (unused days in cycle ÷ days in cycle)

Examples:
- Pro with 300 unused days: $180.00 × 300 ÷ 365 = **$147.95**.
- Basic discontinued with 12 days left in a 30-day cycle: $19.00 × 12 ÷ 30 = **$7.60**.

### 4.3 When Refunds Are Strictly Denied

- Change of mind after day 7.
- Unused days after a normal cancellation.
- Any renewal charge after the 7-day grace period.
- Any Basic monthly charge after day 7, with no partial month credit.
- Downgrades. They never produce credit.
- Pro members who simply stop using perks.

## 5. Non-Refundable Items

- Enterprise setup fee ($1,500.00 one-time).
- Renewal charges after the 7-day grace period.
- NovaCash already redeemed.
- Accounts terminated for fraud or abuse.

## 6. Retention Offers (Save Flows)

### 6.1 Flow Order (as of Sept 30, 2026)

1. The member picks a cancellation reason.
2. **Basic:** sees the 30% discount offer first. If declined, the Pause offer is shown (if eligible).
3. **Pro:** sees the Pause offer, plus an option to switch to Basic when the Pro term ends (no credit).
4. The member can decline everything and confirm the cancellation.

During the Q3 A/B test (July 15–Sept 30, 2026), some Basic members were shown only one of the two offers.

### 6.2 Basic 30% Discount Offer

- **Who:** Basic only.
- **Offer:** 30% off the next 2 billing cycles. That is $5.70 off, so **$13.30 per month**, for a total saving of $11.40. Then the price returns to $19.00.
- **Limit:** Once per account per rolling 12 months.
- **Not available:** During a free trial or after a refund request.
- **Choice:** A member takes either this offer or the pause in a single cancel flow, not both.

### 6.3 60-Day Pause Subscription

- **Who:** Basic and Pro in good standing (no unpaid balance). Not available during a free trial or to Enterprise.
- **Length:** Exactly 60 days, not extendable or splittable.
- **Frequency:** Once per calendar year (Jan 1–Dec 31) per account, counted by pause start date.
- **Timing:** Must start at least 24 hours before the next renewal timestamp.
- **During the pause:**
  - No charges.
  - Perks are suspended. Shipping and cashback revert to Free tier.
  - The NovaCash balance is kept.
  - Existing orders and earned warranty extensions are kept.
- **Billing effect:** A Basic renewal date moves back 60 days. A Pro annual term is extended by 60 days.
- **Resuming:** Automatic after 60 days, with a reminder email 7 days before. A member can resume early, but the remaining days are forfeited and the pause still counts as used for the year.

## 7. Edge Cases

- **Cancel 10 hours before renewal:** the renewal charge processes. A refund can be requested within 7 days if the member has fewer than 2 qualifying perk orders since the charge.
- **Pause already used this year:** the member is not shown the pause offer.
- **Canceling while paused:** allowed. No further charges occur.
- **Pro to Basic downgrade requested mid-term:** effective at term end, no refund.
- **Chargebacks:** the account is suspended while the dispute is open.
