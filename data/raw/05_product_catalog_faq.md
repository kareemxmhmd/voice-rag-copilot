## 3. Customer FAQs

### Q1. How do I file a warranty claim?

File at novacart.example/warranty, in the app, or through support. You need your order number and the product's serial number.

- NovaCart responds within 2 business days.
- Repair, replacement, or refund is at NovaCart's option.
- Repair turnaround is 7 to 10 business days after the unit is received. The prepaid shipping label is free.
- Pro and Enterprise members get advance replacement. It ships within 1 business day of claim approval, with a card hold equal to the item price that is released when the defective unit is scanned within 10 days.

### Q2. What does the warranty cover and exclude?

It covers manufacturing defects from the delivery date through the warranty period in the catalog table.

Battery capacity falling below 80% is covered for 12 months from delivery for all tiers. This is not extended for Pro or Enterprise.

Exclusions:
- Accidental damage (drops, cracked screens).
- Liquid damage beyond the product's water rating.
- Unauthorized repairs.
- Normal wear and cosmetic damage.
- Consumables such as brush heads.
- Loss or theft.

### Q3. Do Pro members get a longer warranty?

Yes. Pro and Enterprise members get **+6 months** on NC-series products bought while their membership is active. For example, the NovaWatch S2 goes from 12 to 18 months.

The extension stays with the product even if the membership later ends.

### Q4. What bundle discounts do Pro members get?

Pro and Enterprise members get **15% off** pre-built bundles. All other members, including Free and Basic, get 8% off. Example: the Work Anywhere Bundle lists at $1,187.00, costs $1,092.04 at 8% off, and costs $1,008.95 for Pro.

### Q5. Can I return just one item from a bundle?

Yes, within the 14-day return window. The item is refunded at its share of the discounted bundle price. The bundle discount on the remaining items is not recalculated. Normal restocking and return shipping rules apply (KB-03).

### Q6. How do stock notifications work?

Tap "Notify Me" on a sold-out product page and choose email, SMS, or push.

- Alerts are sent within 15 minutes of restock.
- Pro and Enterprise members are notified 2 hours before the general list.
- An alert does not reserve stock.
- Alerts expire after 90 days. Each account can have up to 50 active alerts.

### Q7. Which cards can I use for product orders?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover, credit or debit. Prepaid cards from these networks are accepted for product orders.

### Q8. Can I pay with digital wallets?

Yes. Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal are accepted for product orders and subscriptions.

### Q9. How does Buy Now Pay Later work?

NovaPay Later splits a product order into 4 interest-free installments.

- Eligible orders are $50.00 to $1,500.00.
- 25% is due at checkout. The remaining three payments are charged every 14 days.
- Late fee: $7.00 per missed installment.
- Refunds from returns reduce unpaid installments first.
- NovaPay Later is for product orders only. It cannot be used for subscriptions or Enterprise invoices.

### Q10. Can I pay for a subscription with BNPL, gift cards, or NovaCash?

No. Subscriptions accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. Prepaid cards, gift cards, BNPL, and NovaCash are not accepted for subscription charges.
