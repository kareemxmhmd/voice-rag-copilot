# NovaCart Subscription Plans

**Document ID:** KB-01 | **Version:** 3.2 | **Effective:** July 1, 2026 | **Owner:** Membership Product

All prices are in USD and exclude sales tax.

## 1. Plan Overview

NovaCart has four membership tiers: Free, Basic, Pro, and Enterprise.

| Tier | Price | Billing | Seats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | None | 1 |
| Basic | $19.00 per month | Monthly, auto-renews | 1 |
| Pro | $180.00 per year | Annual only, auto-renews | 1 |
| Enterprise | Custom, starting at $499.00 per month | Monthly billing, 12-month minimum term | 10+ |

### 1.1 How Pro's 20% Savings Works

Pro is billed annually only. It is not sold month-to-month.

$180.00 per year equals $15.00 per month. That is 20% less than the $225.00 per year reference rate ($18.75 per month × 12) that NovaCart uses for monthly-billing comparisons. The annual saving is $45.00.

### 1.2 What the Enterprise Price Includes

- The $499.00 per month starting price includes 10 seats (about $49.90 per seat).
- Additional seats cost $45.00 per seat per month.
- The minimum is 10 seats. Quotes for 50 or more seats are custom.
- A one-time Enterprise setup fee of $1,500.00 applies (non-refundable, see KB-02).
- Invoicing is net-30. Accepted payment is ACH or wire.

**NovaCash rules:**
- 1 NovaCash = $1.00.
- It is earned on the merchandise subtotal after discounts, before tax and shipping.
- It is credited 15 days after the delivery date, after the 14-day return window closes.
- It is reversed if the item is returned.
- It expires 12 months after it is credited, or 90 days after membership ends, whichever comes first.
- Minimum redemption is $5.00. It can cover up to 50% of a product order's subtotal.
- It cannot be used to pay for subscription charges.

## 3. Free Trial Rules

- **Who:** New subscribers on Basic and Pro. Enterprise has no free trial.
- **Length:** 14 days (336 hours), starting from the sign-up confirmation timestamp.
- **Limit:** One trial per customer, ever. It can be used on Basic or Pro, not both.
- **Payment method:** Required at sign-up (credit or debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal). No charge during the trial.
- **Auto-conversion:** At the 14-day mark the trial converts to a paid plan. Basic is charged $19.00. Pro is charged $180.00.
- **Cancellation deadline:** To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before day 14. That means by day 13 at the same time of day the trial started.
- **Reminders:** An email is sent on day 10, and an email plus push notification on day 12.
- **Perks during trial:** Full perks of the trial tier apply. NovaCash earned during a trial, and the warranty extension on products bought during it, apply only if the trial converts to a paid plan. If it does not convert, both are voided.
- **Switching during a trial:** Moving between Basic and Pro does not change the trial end date. The new plan's price is charged at the original trial end.

## 4. Upgrades and Proration

Upgrades take effect immediately and trigger an immediate prorated charge.

### 4.1 Proration Formula

- **Credit** = current plan price × (unused days ÷ total days in the current billing period)
- **Charge due now** = new plan price − credit
- Amounts are rounded to the nearest cent.
- The new billing period (monthly or annual) starts on the upgrade date.

### 4.2 Worked Examples (Basic to Pro)

**Example A: 30-day cycle, upgrade on day 10.** 20 days are unused.
- Credit: $19.00 × 20 ÷ 30 = $12.67
- Charge today: $180.00 − $12.67 = **$167.33**
- The new 12-month Pro term starts today.

**Example B: 31-day cycle, 16 days unused.**
- Credit: $19.00 × 16 ÷ 31 = $9.81
- Charge today: $180.00 − $9.81 = **$170.19**

### 4.3 Upgrade Paths

| From → To | What happens |
|---|---|
| Free → Basic | $19.00 charged immediately. No credit. |
| Free → Pro | $180.00 charged immediately. No credit. |
| Basic → Pro | Prorated charge (formula above). |
| Basic → Enterprise | Sales-led. The unused Basic credit uses the same formula and is applied to the first invoice. |
| Pro → Enterprise | Sales-led. Credit = $180.00 × remaining days ÷ 365, applied to the first Enterprise invoice. |

## 5. Downgrades

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. There is no partial credit or refund for unused time.

| From → To | Effective | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pro → Basic | End of the annual Pro term | Pro perks continue until then. Basic then bills $19.00 per month. |
| Pro → Free | End of the annual Pro term | No credit. |
| Basic → Free | Next billing date | Basic perks continue until the cycle ends. No credit. |

- **Deadline:** A downgrade must be requested at least 24 hours before the renewal timestamp. If requested later, it applies at the following renewal and the upcoming renewal charge still applies.
- **Changing your mind:** A scheduled downgrade can be canceled any time before it takes effect. Starting an upgrade also cancels a pending downgrade.

## 6. Enterprise Seat Changes

- **Adding seats:** Takes effect immediately and is prorated daily. Example: adding 4 seats with 10 days left in a 30-day cycle costs 4 × $45.00 × 10 ÷ 30 = $60.00.
- **Removing seats:** Only at renewal. No credit mid-term. The minimum stays at 10 seats.
